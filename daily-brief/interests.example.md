Based in London, and follows the US closely too.

Write these sections, in this order, and leave out any with nothing worth
reading today:

1. **News**: the top UK, US and world stories of the day.
2. **Politics**: UK and US politics: what was decided, not the noise.
3. **Business and markets**: the big business stories, and how the FTSE 100
   and the S&P 500 closed the day before.
4. **Technology**: software, AI and the tools the reader uses.
5. **Outdoors**: hiking and the gear for it.
